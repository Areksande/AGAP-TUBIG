AGAP-TUBIG Safety Guide

SAFE

Water level is within the safe range. Continue monitoring the live reading and stay alert to changes in weather and local advisories.

WARNING

Water level is rising and may become hazardous. Prepare essential items, monitor SMS alerts, and be ready to follow evacuation instructions.

DANGER

Critical water level. Follow local emergency instructions and proceed to the designated evacuation area when instructed.

SMS Alert: After a user logs in with a registered mobile number and address, the system can send warning/danger notifications through the configured SMS service.
