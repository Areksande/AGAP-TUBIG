Safe, Warning, and Danger Levels · Reliable Basis & Risks

SAFE · 0 – 29 cm

Water level is within the normal channel. No immediate risk to people or property.

Reliable basis: Below the warning threshold set during sensor calibration with barangay officials. Possible risks: None to low. Slippery roads may occur during continuous rain. When dangerous: Only if combined with strong currents or structural damage.

WARNING · 30 – 59 cm

Water is rising and may become hazardous. Prepare essential items and be ready to evacuate.

Reliable basis: Threshold based on DRRMO advisories, historical flood records, and barangay memorandums. Possible risks: Moderate flooding of low-lying roads, difficulty for vehicles and pedestrians. When dangerous: If water continues to rise quickly or rain persists, it can escalate to Danger.

DANGER · 60 cm and above

Critical water level. Life-threatening flooding is possible. Evacuate immediately when instructed.

Reliable basis: Critical threshold validated with MDRRMO and barangay rescue teams. Possible risks: High — life-threatening flooding, property damage, need for rescue. When dangerous: Always considered dangerous. Do not attempt to cross floodwaters.

These classifications are supported by official memorandums and references. See the References section below.

DRRMO Coordination · Fast Emergency Response

Automatic Coordination

When the alarm reaches Warning or Danger, the system automatically sends an alert to the MDRRMO and barangay rescue team through the configured SMS and push notification channel.

Real-Time Monitoring

DRRMO personnel can view the same live dashboard and historical data, ensuring a shared source of truth during emergencies.

Rescue Team Dispatch

Upon Danger level, the rescue team receives an immediate alert with the latest water level and sensor location for faster dispatch and response.

DRRMO Coordination Hotline: 0920-617-8343 · 0995-495-4798 · (044) 761 3199

A coordination memorandum with the DRRMO is included in the References section to formalize the alarm-to-response workflow.

Warning Light & Buzzer · Automatic Alerts

Warning Light

The warning light blinks amber at Warning level and turns solid red at Danger level so users can easily notice the alert even from a distance.

Audible Buzzer

A buzzer sounds short intermittent beeps at Warning level and a continuous loud alarm at Danger level. It can be silenced while the visual alert remains active.

Silence Control

Users can silence the buzzer using the Silence button. The warning light and on-screen alert remain visible until the water level returns to Safe.

The warning light and buzzer are activated automatically based on the real-time sensor data and are also triggered during the Test Alarm demonstrations.
